FoodBridge connects food donors with NGOs and volunteers to help redistribute surplus food before it goes to waste.

Donate Food Find Food

How It Works

A simple process to connect surplus food with people in need.

1

Donor lists surplus food

Restaurants, canteens, and event organizers post details about edible surplus food.

2

NGO reserves available food

Registered NGOs and volunteers browse local listings and reserve surplus food items.

3

Food is collected and redistributed

Food is picked up promptly and safely distributed to those in need within the community.
